Welcome to Banff & Jasper!

Turquoise lakes, hanging glaciers and one of the most beautiful roads on Earth. Paddle a red canoe on Lake Louise, drive the Icefields Parkway, stand on the Athabasca Glacier and watch for elk, bears and mountain goats.

    Little tips for big mountains

    🍂

    When to go

    July and August are warmest, with lakes thawed. Late September brings golden larches, and winter brings frozen waterfalls and skating. Moraine Lake road opens late spring.

    🎫

    Park pass

    You need a Parks Canada pass to stop in the national parks. Buy it online or at the gate. Some places, like Moraine Lake, use a shuttle you book ahead.

    🐻

    Wildlife

    Stay 100 m from bears and wolves and 30 m from other animals. Never feed them. Slow down on roads at dawn and dusk.

    🧥

    Layers

    Weather changes fast, even in summer. Pack a warm layer, rain jacket and sun hat. Fuel is rare on the Icefields Parkway, so fill up first.
